Background music goes in this folder.

1. Copy your .mp3 files in here, next to this text file.
2. Open ../../components/resources/Music.qml and add one line per track
   inside the Playlist block, following the ones already there:

       PlaylistItem { source: '../../assets/music/your track.mp3'; }

3. Turn "Background Music" on in the theme's Settings screen.

The playlist is shuffled and looped. If you delete every PlaylistItem line
the playlist is simply empty and the setting has nothing to play - that's a
supported state, nothing will break.
